Hope Center clinic system and website

Client
Hope Center, Erbil
Sector
Mental health & psychosocial support
Role
Sole developer
Scope
Requirements → deployment → training

Hope Center ran its clinical work on paper forms and spreadsheets. I built one secure system for the whole center: patient records, scheduling, clinical documentation, billing, HR and reporting, plus a public website in English, Kurdish and Arabic. Privacy was the hardest requirement, and the deadline was fixed by contract.

11 modules 178 automated test files 16 architecture decision records 2FA for every user
Home screen for each role
Hope Center · clinic system

What I built

  • One permanent record per client, with separate episodes of care in each service line. Record codes never change and are never reused.
  • Scheduling for individual, couple, family and group sessions, repeating series and a waiting list. Bookings are checked for clashes under database locks.
  • Digital versions of the center's own clinical forms. Records move from draft to finalised to locked, and corrections are added as dated addenda.
  • Finance in Iraqi dinars and US dollars on an append-only ledger. A mistake is fixed with a reversal entry; posted rows are never edited.
  • HR with leave balances and licence-expiry warnings, role home screens and a monthly activity report.

Privacy and engineering

  • Clinical notes are readable only by the assigned therapist and the Clinical Director. The rule is enforced in access policies and database queries, not just hidden in the interface.
  • Clinical answers, risk entries, staff ID numbers and salaries are encrypted at rest and never sent to the browser or written to logs.
  • A public website in English, Kurdish and Arabic that sets no cookies, loads nothing from other servers and is rebuilt from code by a setup script.
  • Docker in development, staging and production. Releases deploy by git tag to staging first; backups, health checks and certificates are automated.

How a contract works

The same process for a single client or a company outsourcing part of its workload.

  1. Brief

    You tell me what you need: a system, a module or a list of bugs. I ask questions until the scope is clear.

  2. Scope and quote

    I send a written scope with deliverables, a timeline and a price.

  3. Contract

    We sign a contract that fixes the scope, the deadline, payment terms and code ownership.

  4. Build

    I build and test the work, deploy it to a staging environment, and share progress regularly so there are no surprises.

  5. Delivery

    Production deployment, documentation and handover, with training for your staff where needed. Maintenance is available afterwards.

Working alongside your team

  • I work in your repository and follow your branching, code review and ticket workflow.
  • I match your existing code style, tests and documentation, so the work reads like your own.
  • You get one accountable person for the contract, reachable during your working hours.
